Calendar Rewind

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Calendar Rewind is an iOS app that helps you rediscover experiences from your calendar history, including dining, travel, entertainment, hobbies, and celebrations. We believe your calendar history and personal experiences should remain private.

Calendar Access

With your permission, Calendar Rewind accesses your device's calendars to identify past experiences and display personalized insights and historical summaries.

This includes information such as event titles, dates, locations, notes, calendar details, and participation status.

Calendar Rewind requests Full Access to your calendars, but only reads calendar information. The app does not create, edit, or delete calendar events.

Data Collection and Storage

Calendar Rewind does not collect your personal information or upload your calendar events or generated insights to developer-operated servers.

All calendar analysis is performed on your device using locally stored reference data.

Certain information, including your selected calendar, decisions to confirm or dismiss recognized experiences, and review preferences, is stored locally to support app features. These decisions may include calendar event identifiers.

Calendar event contents and generated insights are not permanently stored by the app.

Analytics and Sharing

Calendar Rewind does not contain advertisements, third-party analytics, or tracking software.

Your calendar information and generated insights are not automatically shared with other apps or third parties.

The app includes optional links to external websites for data attribution. These websites are subject to their own privacy practices.

Your Privacy Choices

You can revoke Calendar Rewind's calendar access through your device's Settings.

Revoking calendar access clears calendar information and generated insights held in the app's memory, but does not automatically delete locally stored preferences or review decisions.

Deleting Calendar Rewind removes its locally stored app data from your device. Your original calendar events remain unchanged, and older device backups may retain copies of some app data.

Calendar Rewind does not require an account.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Any updates will be reflected on this page.
